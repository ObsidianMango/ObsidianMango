How to play

Hit the bullseye. You get three throws on each of three rounds. The best hit in each round counts toward your total.

Open Projectile to choose a fruit or vegetable. You can switch between throws.

Set your bearing, elevation, and power. The dotted line shows the flight path. Later targets move, so lead them as you aim.

Fire with Space. Use the arrow keys to aim, − and + to adjust power, and R for a replay.
