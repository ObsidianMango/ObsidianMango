Kit-car layouts & the original SSK

Parts specialist MG Magic lists separate Gazelle/SSK assembly manuals for Ford, Chevrolet and Volkswagen versions. Similar bodywork can therefore hide different mechanical layouts. The hidden systems in this model demonstrate a generic front-engine, rear-wheel-drive arrangement; they are not an identification of this photographed car.

The original Mercedes-Benz SSK was a different machine. Mercedes’ archive describes a shortened 2,950 mm wheelbase and a supercharged six-cylinder engine of nearly 7.1 litres. “SSK” stands for Super-Sport-Kurz, or Super Sport Short. Those specifications belong to the historic Mercedes, not automatically to a later kit roadster.
